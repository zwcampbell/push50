# Privacy Policy — Push 50

**Last updated: April 26, 2026**

## Overview

Push 50 does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything the app records stays on your device.

## Data We Collect

**None.** Push 50 stores your workout history, program progress, and settings exclusively in on-device storage (Apple SwiftData / iCloud private container). This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server.

## Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, Push 50 uses Apple's local notification system (UNUserNotificationCenter) to schedule alerts on your device. No notification content or preferences are transmitted off-device.

## Third-Party Services

Push 50 uses no third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services.

## Children's Privacy

Push 50 does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer any information to outside parties — because we have no information to transfer.

## Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy in a material way, we will update the "Last updated" date above and submit an updated app to the App Store.
